\subsection{Observation Parameters}

The following parameters define the observation model and its response to the latent states:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Heart Rate:} $\text{HR}_{\text{base}} \approx 62$ bpm, $\kappa_B^{HR} \approx 12$, $\alpha_A^{HR} \approx 3$.
    \item \textbf{Sleep:} $k_C \approx 3$, $k_A \approx 2$, $\tilde{c} \approx 0.5$.
    \item \textbf{Stress:} $S_{\text{base}} \approx 30$, $k_F \approx 20$, $k_A^S \approx 8$.
    \item \textbf{Steps:} $\mu_0^{\text{step}} \approx 5.5$, $\sigma_{\text{st}} \approx 0.5$.
\end{itemize}
For a complete list and their prior distributions, refer to \path{PARAM_PRIOR_CONFIG} in \path{estimation.py}.
